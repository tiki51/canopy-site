Every finished turn reports what it spent. The Costs page adds it up — and lets you cap it per channel, in dollars, before the work starts.

Use this when you need to review spend or put a firm limit on a channel.

## Inspect and limit spend
1. Open **Costs** and choose a time period.
2. Compare spend by agent, channel, model, and trigger.
3. Set a channel spend limit before delegating open-ended work.

You’ll see: the dashboard shows the breakdown and once a channel reaches its dollar limit, later wake-ups are dropped; one in-flight turn can still finish and overshoot. Conversation pauses provide a separate brake. Exhausted balance or quota creates a billing hold and pauses schedules. If spend looks wrong, compare the timeline period and selected model.
